The Solution

Plan it. Climb it. Prove it.

A calendar-first accountability loop that makes follow-through visible.

Calendar tells you WHAT

Google Calendar is the source of truth. Real events, not user-typed goals.

Camera tells you HOW

03 / 12
CompCal
How Product Works

The mountain isn't decoration.
It's the entire interface.

Mountain as Information Architecture

Two-Layer Focus Verification

LLM — Claude Haiku Vision, periodic JPEG checks, distraction 0 to 1

CV — MediaPipe + OpenCV, continuous gaze at 30fps over WebSocket

Features Shipped at Hackathon

Full Calendar to Mountain to Summit flow with timer

Friends screen with invite links and live climbing status

Snowball throw — throw at friends less focused than you

Trust toggles and low-pressure mode

Real-time eye-tracking over WebSocket (prototype)

Design Philosophy

Support, not shame. The mountain stalls — it doesn't fall off a cliff. Built for the realistic student, not the productivity maximalist.
